# Language reference

Aafno’s agreed long-term reference tools help learners look up a word, understand a form, and practise using it without stepping through a course. The three tools draw on the same reviewed language content as lessons.

## Grammar Library

Search by construction, form, or communicative need, such as how to speak respectfully about a past event. Each entry can explain how a construction is formed, what it communicates and where it is appropriate. Include contextual examples, comparisons with related constructions, linked conjugation tables and targeted practice. Use the [grammar syllabus](../learning/grammar-syllabus.md) to organise entries and link recurring forms to their canonical concepts.

## Verb Conjugation Reference

Search by lemma or inflected form. Let learners explore forms by pronoun, person, number and gender where relevant, tense and aspect, honorific level or register, and positive or negative polarity. Identify irregular forms and show validated contextual examples. Link forms to their dictionary entries, relevant grammar explanations and practice. Explain the construction in Nepali usage terms with approximate English glosses.

Include a selectable [Highest honorific (Ati Uchcha Aadar)](../learning/highest-honorific.md) register and distinguish reviewed full and contracted variants wherever supported. Investigate every applicable verb and construction across tense and aspect, negation, questions, imperatives, modality, compounds, complex sentences and evidentiality. Show contextual usage tags such as family use, validated examples and audio from speakers who naturally use the register. Use reviewed variants in assessment when the prompt context permits them. Keep unverified forms as unspecified research gaps. See the [concept framework](../learning/grammar-concept-framework.md#highest-honorific-across-conjugations) and [Content QA](../learning/content-qa.md).

## Nepali Dictionary

Support bidirectional English–Nepali lookup and searches in English, Romanised Nepali and Devanagari. Use exact matches, reviewed aliases and fuzzy matching for spelling variation and mistyped queries. A search for *ramro*, *raamro* or राम्रो can surface the same entry. An inflected lookup such as *garchhu* can lead to गर्नु and explain the matched form. Rank plausible alternatives when the spelling, form or sense is ambiguous and show what each result matched.

An entry can include a headword, part of speech, definitions for multiple senses, translations, pronunciation and native-speaker audio where available, contextual examples, and register notes. Connect synonyms, antonyms, collocations and derived words by sense where appropriate. Link relevant grammar and conjugation entries. Learners can save words and add entries or individual senses to personal flashcards.

Show Devanagari and Romanisation together by default. Romanisation has an independent display toggle that leaves Devanagari and the literacy pathway available. The display preference does not restrict lookup in any supported input format.

## Shared language content

The tools, lessons, exercises, flashcards and eventual approved AI role-play draw on one validated knowledge base. Shared content includes lexical entries and senses, grammar concepts, forms, contextual examples and audio. Reviewed versions, provenance and register notes travel with reused content. Role-play delivery remains subject to the approval described in [AI integration](../technical/ai-integration.md).

An illustrative journey starts with गर्नु in the dictionary. The learner opens its conjugation reference, selects a respectful past construction, follows its grammar explanation, practises in context, and saves the verb to a personal flashcard. Lookup and saving are exposure or study actions. Mastery requires skill-specific evidence under the [curriculum structure](../learning/curriculum-structure.md).

## Licensing and publication

Use licensed lexical sources or original reviewed entries. Record source and reuse rights for definitions, examples, translations and audio. Unlicensed scraping and publication of unvalidated AI output are excluded. Apply [Content QA](../learning/content-qa.md) to every learner-facing version. The lookup examples and form labels on this page are illustrative drafts pending native-speaker and linguistic expert validation.
