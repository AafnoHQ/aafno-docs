# Grammar catalogue reconciliation

Phase 2A.2 Step 2 reconciles the 527 provisional coverage targets recorded at [baseline 3a103ce](https://github.com/AafnoHQ/aafno-docs/commit/3a103ce954fade63cbd8e0997485eb52309fae2d). All five review batches have structural approval. The implementation is under pull request review. The catalogue contains **500 planned learner targets across 31 categories**. Research-dependent targets remain provisional, and linguistic and native-speaker validation is pending.

The review compared observable learner evidence, grammatical formation, interpretation, contextual selection and productive application. A concept can remain independent while sharing its language specification with another concept. Reference inventories, attestation and contextual variation belong on the relevant forms and shared assets.

## Dispositions

- **Retain independently** keeps a distinct learner outcome under its original ID.
- **Canonical owner** identifies one of seven priority owners of shared grammatical knowledge.
- **Cross-reference** preserves the learner outcome and links related concepts.
- **Merged alias** keeps the original ID as a permanent lookup and moves its learner outcome to one or more surviving destinations.
- **Shared orientation** keeps explanations searchable for the Grammar Library and lesson introductions, with content validation and links to evidence-bearing concepts.
- **Retained with approved clarification** records an approved assessment boundary under the existing ID.

The 500 planned targets include 256 independently retained targets, seven priority canonical owners, 201 cross-referenced targets and 36 retained with clarified boundaries. The 22 merged aliases and five shared orientation records remain discoverable through the original topics and traceability pages. No concept IDs were added or renumbered.

[Highest honorific (Ati Uchcha Aadar)](../highest-honorific.md) covers formation, commands and requests, social choice and household variation through separate learner outcomes. Full and contracted forms are proposed variants of one register. The shared register evidence applies to relevant constructions across categories.

Use a single reviewed account for each form across the Grammar Library, Verb Conjugation Reference, Dictionary, lessons and activities, flashcards, assessment, and AI-assisted conversation or roleplay. Shared assets carry provenance, review status and contextual usage. Placement, production and selection can supply independent learner evidence when their assessment tasks differ.
