# Connecting ideas and constructing complex sentences

[Grammar catalogue](index.md) · Phase 2A.2 Step 2

Product coverage is agreed for all 114 concepts in G19–G24. IDs are provisional until full catalogue reconciliation. All classifications, Nepali examples, Romanisation and English glosses are drafts pending linguistic and native-speaker validation. All detailed concept specifications are **Planned** for Step 3.

The examples illustrate how learners can connect ideas, explain decisions, describe experiences, tell stories, report conversations and discuss hypothetical situations. The demonstrations and assessment illustrations below are static documentation of draft learning designs.

### Connecting two ideas

| Nepali | Romanisation | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| म खाना खान्छु र चिया पिउँछु। | Ma khaana khaanchhu ra chiyaa piunchhu. | I eat food and drink tea. |
| म जान चाहन्छु तर समय छैन। | Ma jaana chaahanchhu tara samaya chhaina. | I want to go, but I don’t have time. |
| म घर जान्छु किनभने म थाकेको छु। | Ma ghar jaanchhu kinabhane ma thaakeko chhu. | I’m going home because I’m tired. |
| म थाकेको छु त्यसैले घर जान्छु। | Ma thaakeko chhu tyasaile ghar jaanchhu. | I’m tired, so I’m going home. |
| पानी परिरहेको छ तैपनि म फुटबल खेल्न जान्छु। | Paani pariraheko chha taipani ma football khelna jaanchhu. | It’s raining. Even so, I’m going to play football. |

किनभने introduces the reason. त्यसैले introduces the consequence.

### Combining actions

| Stage | Nepali | Romanisation | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Separate action | मैले खाना खाएँ। | Maile khaana khaaẽ. | I ate food. |
| Separate action | म घर गएँ। | Ma ghar gaẽ. | I went home. |
| Combined | म खाना खाएर घर गएँ। | Ma khaana khaayera ghar gaẽ. | I went home after eating. |

The finite खाएँ becomes the sequential participle खाएर. The separately marked subject मैले becomes a shared म in this example.

### Reporting perspective

| Perspective | Nepali | Romanisation | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Original speaker | म भोलि आउँछु। | Ma bholi aauchhu. | I’ll come tomorrow. |
| Reporter | उसले भोलि आउँछु भन्यो। | Usle bholi aauchhu bhanyo. | He said he would come tomorrow. |

The embedded आउँछु preserves the original speaker’s first-person perspective. Interpret person, time and place in the quotation and reporting contexts. English pronoun and tense shifts do not determine the Nepali forms.

### Evidential contrasts

| Nepali | Romanisation | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ऊ भोलि आउँछ। | U bholi aauchha. | He's coming tomorrow. |
| ऊ भोलि आउँछ रे। | U bholi aauchha re. | Apparently, he's coming tomorrow. |
| ऊ भोलि आउला। | U bholi aaula. | He might come tomorrow. |

These examples illustrate an unmarked assertion, reported information with रे, and a possibility. G23 covers constructing reports. G26 and G27 cover particle behaviour and the interpretation of information source, inference, certainty and discovery.

| Base action | बच्चाले खाना खान्छ। | Bachchaale khaana khaanchha. | The child eats food. |

The child eats in both examples. The causative introduces Mother as causer, marked by ले, and marks the child as causee with लाई. खाना is the food in both constructions.

### Agreement and perspective in reported speech

| Nepali | Romanisation | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| आमाले भोलि आउनुहुन्छ भन्नुभयो। | Aamaale bholi aaunuhunchha bhannubhayo. | Mother said that someone referred to respectfully would come tomorrow. Context identifies who is coming. |

In this draft standard respectful example, आउनुहुन्छ agrees with the person coming in the reported content. भन्नुभयो expresses respect for Mother as the person who said it. The two verbs can have different referents. Establish the original speaker, the reporter, the addressee and the person referred to before interpreting register in either clause.

Validate genuine family-register variation and record any attested invariant forms, including connectors and non-finite forms. Keep unknown family honorific forms as research gaps. Use attested forms and establish their agreement and perspective in context.
