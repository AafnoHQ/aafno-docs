Linguistic review covers case marking, auxiliary agreement, future and perfect constructions, and natural spoken usage.

### Teaching contrasts as independent concepts

Understanding how to form two constructions does not necessarily mean understanding when to choose between them.

For example:

| Construction | Nepali | Romanisation | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| Habitual | म फुटबल खेल्छु। | Ma football khelchhu. | I play football. |
| Progressive | म फुटबल खेल्दै छु। | Ma football kheldai chhu. | I am playing football. |
| Past habitual | म फुटबल खेल्थेँ। | Ma football khelthẽ. | I used to play football. |

Past habitual forms do not necessarily imply that a habit has stopped without additional context.

G09-017 specifically assesses contextual selection between habitual and progressive meanings. This is a distinct learning outcome from conjugating each construction independently.

The other approved contrast concepts likewise assess contextual selection.

| Nepali | English |
|---|---|
