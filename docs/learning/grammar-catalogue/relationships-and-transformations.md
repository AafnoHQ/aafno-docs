### Draft case marking examples

| Nepali | Romanisation | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| रामले खाना खायो। | Ramle khaana khaayo. | Ram ate food. |
| मैले कलमले लेखेँ। | Maile kalamle lekhẽ. | I wrote with a pen. |

| Part of the second sentence | Romanisation | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| मैले | Maile | Agent |
| कलमले | Kalamle | Instrument |
| लेखेँ | Lekhẽ | Verb |

The two occurrences of ले mark different roles in the second sentence.

The locative selection shows घरमा (Gharma, at home or in the house) in म घरमा छु। (Ma gharma chhu. I am at home.) This illustrates how a Grammar Library reference could show the effect of a marker in context.

### Draft comparison patterns

| Construction | Nepali | Romanisation | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic description | यो घर ठूलो छ। | Yo ghar thulo chha. | This house is big. |
| Comparative | यो घर त्यो घरभन्दा ठूलो छ। | Yo ghar tyo gharbhanda thulo chha. | This house is bigger than that house. |
| Superlative | यो घर सबैभन्दा ठूलो छ। | Yo ghar sabaibhanda thulo chha. | This house is the biggest. |
| Equality | यो घर त्यो घर जत्तिकै ठूलो छ। | Yo ghar tyo ghar jattikai thulo chha. | This house is as big as that house. |
| Intensification | यो घर धेरै ठूलो छ। | Yo ghar dherai thulo chha. | This house is very big. |

The visual exercise prototype illustrates comparative, superlative and equality readings. Its draft comparative example is घर B, घर A भन्दा ठूलो छ। (Ghar B, ghar A bhanda thulo chha. House B is bigger than House A.) The visual format could provide evidence of comprehension without relying solely on English translations.

### Draft proportional comparison

जति धेरै अभ्यास गर्छौ, त्यति राम्रो बोल्न सक्छौ।

Jati dherai abhyaas garchhau, tyati ramro bolna sakchhau.

The more you practise, the better you can speak.

This example combines comparison, quantity, modality and correlative clause formation.

| Construction | Research focus |
| --- | --- |

| Negative imperatives | Negative commands and requests, including their agreement and register. |
