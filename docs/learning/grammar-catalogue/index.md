## What counts as a concept

A concept has a distinct, observable outcome that can be taught and assessed independently. Lessons can combine concepts, and pathways can revisit the same concept at progressively greater depth. Vocabulary supplies words learners use in constructions and has its own scope in [Phase 2C](../curriculum-roadmap.md#phase-2--master-syllabus). Proficiency levels describe broader ability and are assigned in later progression work.

Give each shared concept one canonical entry and link its applications from related categories. Keep examples, form variants and deeper practice together when they serve the same outcome. Give an extension its own entry when it adds an independently teachable and assessable outcome. Record overlaps for reconciliation while preserving the agreed entries.
