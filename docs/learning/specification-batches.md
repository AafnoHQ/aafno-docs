# Specification batches

Batch membership, structural review status and PR references are maintained in the status manifest. See the [generated tracker](specification-progress.md) for per-concept status and canonical paths.

## Step 3A

Pilot consolidation covers seven existing specifications across sentence structure, conjugation, tense and aspect, case, experiencers, hearsay and family variation. See the [pilot index](concepts/index.md#step-3a-pilots) for scope and [PR #9](https://github.com/AafnoHQ/aafno-docs/pull/9) for the merged implementation and structural review. Linguistic and native-speaker review remain open. The PR contains the pilot QA record. Decisions SPEC-01–07 and PILOT-01–04 govern this work.

## Step 3B Batch 1

The first authoring batch covers eight G01 sentence structure targets and four G02 personal pronoun targets. [Subject omission](concepts/subject-omission.md) is the existing related G01 pilot. See the [Batch 1 index](concepts/index.md#step-3b-batch-1) for canonical scope. The tracker and linked PR record authoring, structural review and QA results. Decisions 3B-01–09 and B1-01–11 apply.

Open research includes functions versus roles, word-order interpretation, clause boundaries, pronoun reference and group agreement. See the [research index](specification-research.md). [PR #10](https://github.com/AafnoHQ/aafno-docs/pull/10) merged following product-owner structural review. That review covered completeness, canonical ownership, assessment boundaries and the reusable authoring process.

## Step 3B Batch 2

The [Batch 2 index](concepts/index.md#step-3b-batch-2) records the six remaining pronoun and eight noun specifications. The merged Batch 1 implementation is the authoring baseline. Batch 2 is drafted and awaiting product-owner review. Linguistic and native-speaker review remains open, CEFR is unassigned, and no example is approved for publication.

Decisions B2-01–13 govern scope, ownership boundaries, depth and the two [product capability extensions](../product/language-reference.md#reference-explorer-extension). The [tracker](specification-progress.md) derives the per-concept statuses, and the [research index](specification-research.md#batch-2-research-themes) groups the evidence still needed.
