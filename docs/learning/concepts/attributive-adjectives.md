## 2. Learning outcomes

- LO1: Identify an adjective within a noun phrase.
- LO2: Identify the noun it modifies.
- LO3: Distinguish a descriptive phrase from a quality assertion.
- LO4: Produce a reviewed noun phrase with a description.

## 3. Meaning and usage

An attributive adjective is part of an expression that names or identifies an entity. The expression ‘a nice house’ can be used inside a larger utterance while leaving the quality inside the noun phrase.

## 4. Formation rules

Start with the entity ‘house’, add the quality before the noun in the illustrated pattern and optionally add the demonstrative to identify that house. Contrast the phrase with the sentence that asserts the quality.

**Worked models.** राम्रो घर / Raamro ghar / A nice house (An option on a property list.) Compare त्यो राम्रो घर / Tyo raamro ghar / That nice house (A demonstrative narrows a noun phrase.) The examples are instructional candidates.

## 5. Variations and agreement

Treat arrangement as a reviewed construction rather than a universal position rule. Demonstrative selection, agreement, possession and combining several adjectives remain separate.

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

A learner can sound plausible while using a cue unrelated to the target. Test what they inferred with the two independent prompts in section 12.

**Corrective cue for the first prompt.** Bracket the entire noun phrase and point to the noun inside it.

**Corrective cue for the second prompt.** If you produced a full assertion, reduce it to the label needed on the property list and retry.

Use the [variant-aware assessment policy](../proficiency-framework.md#variant-aware-assessment) if context permits more than one answer or the variant has not been reviewed.

## 12. Activity types

References to rows are to section 6. Provide Devanagari, Romanisation and English by default. For unaided production, hide examples and target expressions until after the response. A scaffolded response is recorded as guided practice.

### First evidence target

- **Learner prompt.** Which expression in row 2 is described as nice?
- **Expected response.** घर / Ghar / house.
- **Assessment target.** LO2 only. Score this response independently of the other activities.
- **Feedback and retry.** Bracket the entire noun phrase and point to the noun inside it.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, context and acceptable responses before scored use.

### Second evidence target

- **Learner prompt.** Label a house as a nice house in Nepali using a noun phrase. Hide the models.
- **Expected response.** राम्रो घर / Raamro ghar / a nice house.
- **Assessment target.** LO4 only. Score this response independently of the other activities.
- **Feedback and retry.** If you produced a full assertion, reduce it to the label needed on the property list and retry.
