## 3. Meaning and usage

Agreement concerns the adjective form licensed in a construction. A noun’s natural or grammatical gender and number interpretation provide possible cues; identifying either cue is separate from applying the adjective pattern.

## 4. Formation rules

Compare rows 1 and 2 using a known referent. Then compare the single and multiple referent in rows 1 and 3. Keep the adjective lexeme constant. Rows 4–6 show why acknowledging number need not entail changing the adjective.

**Worked models.** राम्रो केटा / Raamro keta / A good boy (Singular illustrative frame.) Compare राम्री केटी / Raamri keti / A good girl (Same quality with a different noun frame.) The examples are instructional candidates.

## 5. Variations and agreement

Review gender-related, ordinary plural, unmarked-contextual plural and respectful references separately. An honorific verb or an overt noun suffix is not sufficient evidence of an adjective form. Treat unresolved agreement contexts as draft research.

### Agreement limits to investigate

Request matched examples for feminine plural, inanimate nouns, mixed groups and plural reference without visible noun marking. Change just one condition at a time. In honorific contexts compare social address and verb morphology with adjective choice rather than using one as a proxy for another. Compare a noun phrase and a predicate with the same adjective and referent before assuming the same pattern.

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

A learner can sound plausible while using a cue unrelated to the target. Test what they inferred with the two independent prompts in section 12.

**Corrective cue for the first prompt.** Keep the noun supplied; compare the agreement environment and choose the descriptor form again.

**Corrective cue for the second prompt.** Use the supplied group cue, then compare the adjective with the singular form. Do not change the noun.

Use the [variant-aware assessment policy](../proficiency-framework.md#variant-aware-assessment) if context permits more than one answer or the variant has not been reviewed.

## 12. Activity types

References to rows are to section 6. Provide Devanagari, Romanisation and English by default. For unaided production, hide examples and target expressions until after the response. A scaffolded response is recorded as guided practice.

### First evidence target

- **Learner prompt.** Given the intended referent and noun for ‘girl’, choose the adjective for ‘good’ from the reviewed forms.
- **Expected response.** राम्री / Raamri / good, in this frame.
- **Assessment target.** LO2 only. Provide the noun and pertinent context; do not credit noun gender recognition or noun-number formation.
- **Feedback and retry.** Keep the noun supplied; compare the agreement environment and choose the descriptor form again.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, context and acceptable responses before scored use.

### Second evidence target

- **Learner prompt.** The noun for ‘boys’ is already supplied and the scene clearly has several boys. Choose the adjective form for ‘good’.
- **Expected response.** राम्रा / Raamraa / good, in this frame.
- **Assessment target.** LO3 only. Provide the noun and pertinent context; do not credit noun gender recognition or noun-number formation.
- **Feedback and retry.** Use the supplied group cue, then compare the adjective with the singular form. Do not change the noun.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, context and acceptable responses before scored use.

### Additional evidence target

- **Learner prompt.** With the plural noun already supplied, give only the adjective for ‘beautiful’. The earlier singular example is hidden.
- **Expected response.** सुन्दर / Sundar / beautiful, unchanged in this reviewed frame.
- **Assessment target.** LO4 only. Provide the noun and pertinent context; do not credit noun gender recognition or noun-number formation.
- **Feedback and retry.** If you added an ending to mirror number, compare the descriptor in the matched examples, hide them again and retry with the plural noun supplied.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, context and acceptable responses before scored use.

## 13. Mastery evidence

Supply noun and referent information independently. Score the selected singular gender form and number form separately. Include a constant descriptor and an honorific case that calls for clarification rather than an unsupported answer. Record what learners actually identify, interpret, select or produce against the applicable local outcome. Keep uncertainty visible and avoid inferring one outcome from evidence for another.
