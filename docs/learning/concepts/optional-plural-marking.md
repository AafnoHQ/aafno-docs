## 2. Learning outcomes

- LO1: Infer plurality from numeral and classifier.
- LO2: Infer plurality from a quantifier or discourse.
- LO3: Distinguish unambiguous and unresolved number readings.
- LO4: Compare marked and unmarked options in context.
- LO5: Justify a choice using contextual cues.

## 3. Meaning and usage

Numerals, quantifiers and shared discourse can communicate plurality while the noun remains unmarked. Explicit marking can help when context leaves the number open.

## 4. Formation rules

Compare three counted books, many books and an explicitly plural form. Then use the bare request after establishing one book or three selected books. Look for the cue in the preceding conversation before changing the noun.

**Worked models.** तीनवटा किताब / Tinwata kitaab / Three books Compare धेरै किताब / Dherai kitaab / Many books Use the situations in section 6 to establish the intended readings.

## 5. Variations and agreement

Avoid blanket rules requiring a suffix for plurality or banning numeral-plus-marked forms. Specificity, emphasis and grouping are candidate factors to investigate, not rules governing every context.

### Marking comparison

Contrast तीनवटा किताब / Tinwata kitaab / three books with the research candidate तीनवटा किताबहरू / Tinwata kitaabharu / three books with explicit noun plural marking. Elicit both in an out-of-the-blue order and when the speaker is selecting a known subset. Treat judgements about added emphasis, grouping or naturalness as open. Do not score the candidate as correct or incorrect until its usage in that situation is reviewed.

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

**Likely misunderstanding.** A learner may remove or add the marker mechanically without recovering the quantity from discourse.

**Retry.** Count the requested items and locate the counting expression. For the contrast target, replay the previous turn and identify the known set. Mark uncertainty if it is missing. Use the [variant-aware assessment policy](../proficiency-framework.md#variant-aware-assessment) for contextual mismatches and unreviewed forms.

## 12. Activity types

Row references below refer to the contextual examples in section 6. Use all three language representations by default; adjust visibility only for the evidence target.

### Initial target

- **Learner prompt.** What conveys plurality in the counted-books phrase?
- **Expected response.** तीनवटा / Tinwata / three with classifier, without an explicit suffix on the noun.
- **Assessment target.** LO1. Record the response against this target separately from the other prototype.
- **Feedback and retry.** Count the requested items and locate the counting expression.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Review the scenario, intended reading and accepted responses before use.

### Contrast target

- **Learner prompt.** What would the final request mean after one book was set aside, and after three?
- **Expected response.** One or three respectively if context supports it; the wording alone does not fix the count.
- **Assessment target.** LO2 and LO3. Record the response against this target separately from the other prototype.
- **Feedback and retry.** Replay the previous turn and identify the known set. Mark uncertainty if it is missing.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Review the scenario, intended reading and accepted responses before use.
