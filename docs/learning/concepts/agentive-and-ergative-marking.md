## 2. Learning outcomes

- LO1: Recognise agentive ले.
- LO2: Identify the relevant agent.
- LO3: Attach the marker appropriately to familiar nouns and pronouns.
- LO4: Construct validated agentive-marking patterns.
- LO5: Distinguish marked and unmarked constructions.
- LO6: Distinguish agentive and instrumental functions.
- LO7: Transfer established patterns to unfamiliar validated contexts.

## 3. Meaning and usage

रामले खाना खायो।

Raamle khaana khaayo.

Ram ate food.

म खाना खान्छु।

Ma khaana khaanchhu.

I eat food.

मैले खाना खाएँ।

Maile khaana khaaẽ.

I ate food.

These illustrate contrasts involving participant roles and grammatical constructions.

Do not teach that ले is simply a past-tense subject marker.

## 4. Formation rules

Illustrative noun attachment:

| Base | Marked form |
|---|---|
| राम — Raam | रामले — Raamle |
| सीता — Sitaa | सीताले — Sitaale |
| दाइ — Daai | दाइले — Daaile |
| आमा — Aamaa | आमाले — Aamaale |
| साथी — Saathi | साथीले — Saathile |
| शिक्षक — Shikshak | शिक्षकले — Shikshakle |

Pronoun transformations may differ from ordinary noun attachment.

म — Ma — I.

मैले — Maile — I, agentive-marked.

Do not teach मले (Male) as the standard transformation.

Canonical pronoun case transformations belong to G14-024.

Contrast:

मैले खाना खाएँ।

Maile khaana khaaẽ.

I ate food.

म घर गएँ।

Ma ghar gaẽ.

I went home.

Not every past event uses ले.

Detailed split-ergative conditions belong to G14-026.

## 5. Variations and agreement

Draft pronoun transformations:

| Base | Agentive-marked form |
|---|---|
| म — Ma | मैले — Maile |
| तँ — Ta | तैँले — Taile |
| तिमी — Timi | तिमीले — Timile |
| तपाईं — Tapaai | तपाईंले — Tapaaile |
| हजुर — Hajur | हजुरले — Hajurle |
| ऊ — U | उसले — Usle |
| उनी — Uni | उनले — Unle |
| उहाँ — Uhã | उहाँले — Uhãle |
| हामी — Haami | हामीले — Haamile |
| उनीहरू — Uniharu | उनीहरूले — Uniharule |

Validate all transformations.

Register illustrations:

तिमीले खाना खायौ।

Timile khaana khaayau.

You ate food.

तपाईंले खाना खानुभयो।

Tapaaile khaana khaanubhayo.

You ate food, respectfully.

हजुरले खाना खाइसियो।

Hajurle khaana khaisiyo.

You ate food, highest honorific.

The final example requires highest-honorific validation.

Do not assume हजुरले uniquely determines a conjugation pattern.

## 7. Related constructions and contrasts

Agentive:

रामले चिठी लेख्यो।

Raamle chithi lekhyo.

Ram wrote a letter.

Instrumental:

राम कलमले लेख्छ।

Raam kalamle lekhchha.

Ram writes with a pen.

Combined:

रामले कलमले चिठी लेख्यो।

Raamle kalamle chithi lekhyo.

Ram wrote a letter with a pen.

The same surface marker can express different functions.

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

Address:

- Omitting required marking.
- Applying ले to every past-tense subject.
- Incorrect pronoun transformation.
- Interpreting every ले as "with".
- Confusing case marking with agreement.
- Rejecting legitimate unmarked constructions.

Use contrasts involving म (Ma), मैले (Maile) and मलाई (Malaai).

**Feedback and retry.** Ask who performed the action and whether the phrase could be naming an instrument. Then give the predicate and aspect as cues to reconsider the marking. Ask the learner to choose among म / Ma / I, मैले / Maile / agent-marked I, and मलाई / Malaai / dative or object-marked me.

## 9. Natural usage

आज के गर्‍यौ?

Aaja ke garyau?

What did you do today?

फुटबल खेलेँ।

Football khelẽ.

I played football.

कसले जित्यो?

Kasle jityo?

Who won?

हामीले जित्यौँ।

Haamile jityaũ.

We won.

This dialogue also illustrates subject omission and interrogative marking.

## 10. Prerequisites

Sentence components and relevant verb meanings are required.

Pronouns are helpful.

Transitivity may be co-taught initially and becomes important for broader independent application.

Pronoun transformations may be co-taught.

Tense, aspect and register depend on the activity.

Subject omission is helpful.

Devanagari literacy is not required.
