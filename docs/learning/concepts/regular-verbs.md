## 3. Meaning and usage

Regularity is predictability within a specified frame or class. A shared pattern for first-person habitual forms can support transfer within that frame, but does not classify the entire paradigms of the verbs.

## 4. Formation rules

Hold person and habitual context fixed. Compare read and write with their supplied stems. Then give the learner a new class member and stem with its result hidden. Check the produced form against the reviewed model and restrict the inference to that frame.

**Worked illustrations.** पढ्नु / पढ्छु / Padhnu / Padhchhu / To read / I read (Consonant-final stem in the first-person habitual draft model.) Compare लेख्नु / लेख्छु / Lekhnu / Lekhchhu / To write / I write (Same reviewed-draft pattern in this frame.)

## 5. Variations and agreement

Predictable spelling or sound alternations can be part of the supported pattern. An exceptional form elsewhere does not invalidate the local comparison. Use register and class information when comparing frames without building a universal paradigm. Relevant validated register comparisons may eventually extend this work. [G08-005](highest-honorific-conjugation-and-agreement.md) owns highest-honorific conjugation and agreement. G12-004, G25-005 and G31-007 retain imperative formation, social selection and family usage. Do not derive family forms from standard respectful forms.

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

**Diagnose the interpretation.** Point to the two recurring forms as pronounced, then try the comparison with खेल्छु / Khelchhu / I play.

**Retry without leaking the target.** If you copied the other verb’s base, hold the grammatical frame steady and replace only the lexical base. Retry with another reviewed class member. Use [variant-aware assessment](../proficiency-framework.md#variant-aware-assessment) when a plausible response depends on a reading or form still under review.

## 9. Natural usage

**Draft conversational scene.** A learner hears several people report routines and is given a new verb to use in the same first-person habitual frame. Pause if the conversation changes to a different tense or register. Use the matching contextual utterances in section 6 and review their naturalness in the exchange.

## 12. Activity types

Row references are to section 6. Provide Devanagari, Romanisation and English by default. For scored work, hide any model answer, full gloss, row label or annotation that discloses the target. Keep the Nepali forms that learners must interpret or recognise visible. Supply lexical meanings without revealing the target form relationship or grammatical interpretation.

### LO1 — Recognise the shared pattern in a reviewed frame

- **Learner prompt.** Compare the Nepali read and write pairs with the full glosses hidden. Which material recurs in the two first-person habitual forms?
- **Expected response.** Both forms have the same spoken ending, chhu. The lexical bases differ. Confirm the exact written segmentation before treating it as a morphological analysis.
- **Assessment target.** LO1 only. Assess pattern transfer within the supplied construction, separately from verb vocabulary, habitual interpretation and detailed agreement production under G08 and G09.
- **Feedback and retry.** Point to the two recurring forms as pronounced, then try the comparison with खेल्छु / Khelchhu / I play.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm the example, analysis and accepted responses before use.

### LO2 — Produce a new form by transferring a supplied pattern

- **Learner prompt.** Given बोल् / Bol / speak stem and its membership in the supplied first-person habitual class, produce the comparable form. Show only the read and write models; hide the answer and all speak examples.
- **Expected response.** बोल्छु / Bolchhu / I speak.
- **Assessment target.** LO2 only. Assess pattern transfer within the supplied construction, separately from verb vocabulary, habitual interpretation and detailed agreement production under G08 and G09.
- **Feedback and retry.** If you copied the other verb’s base, hold the grammatical frame steady and replace only the lexical base. Retry with another reviewed class member.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm the example, analysis and accepted responses before use.

### LO3 — Distinguish supported class transfer from extrapolation to another construction

- **Learner prompt.** Compare पढ्छु / Padhchhu and लेख्छु / Lekhchhu in routine reports, then जान्छु / Jaanchhu and गएँ / Gaẽ in routine and completed-trip reports. Which actual forms support transfer within the supplied habitual frame, and what does the go pair show about applying that pattern to a past report? Hide full glosses.
- **Expected response.** The read and write forms share the habitual ending in this frame. जान्छु / Jaanchhu / I go and गएँ / Gaẽ / I went share an entry, but the past example is not made by applying the habitual pattern. A past model or class evidence is needed.
- **Assessment target.** LO3 only. Score a form-based explanation of the transfer limit. G08/G09 retain inflection and tense interpretation.
- **Feedback and retry.** If the learner carries the habitual ending into the completed-trip report, compare both go forms and identify which one belongs to which scenario. Retry by evaluating a claim that the read pattern alone supplies the past of the write verb.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm both the analysis and accepted responses with speakers before scored use.

### LO4 — Identify missing information needed for transfer

- **Learner prompt.** The candidate is नाच्नु / Naachnu / to dance. Show the read and write habitual models without any dance inflection or class label. What would you check before applying their first-person habitual pattern to this entry?
- **Expected response.** Check a supported stem for this construction and evidence that the verb follows the same pattern in the same person, construction and register. Accept a reviewed same-frame form or documented class membership with relevant stem evidence. The dictionary ending alone is insufficient.
- **Assessment target.** LO4 only. Score identifying the missing evidence for this candidate. Do not score producing its inflected form.
- **Feedback and retry.** If the learner relies on the shared ending, ask whether that shows the same stem behaviour and habitual inflection. Retry by choosing between an unrelated past example and a same-frame reference.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Review the candidate and class membership before any transfer task. This item does not assert the candidate belongs to the class.
