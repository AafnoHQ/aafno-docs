## 2. Learning outcomes

- LO1: Identify the quantity a cardinal expresses.
- LO2: Distinguish numerical value from digit script or grouping.
- LO3: Interpret lakh and crore in context.
- LO4: Produce an exact quantity in a supplied frame.

## 3. Meaning and usage

Cardinals give a count or the magnitude of a measurement. Digits, grouping and spoken wording represent a value in different ways. Written grouping may change the visual pattern without changing the numerical value.

## 4. Formation rules

Read the digits without separators, regroup from the right, and match the result to the supplied value. In South Asian grouping, the rightmost group has three digits and preceding groups have two. Match 100,000 to one lakh and 10,000,000 to one crore. Then supply a small exact cardinal in a phrase whose unit is already given.

**Worked models.** तीन / Tin / Three (Value 3 or ३; count the books when used with the supplied book-counting frame.) Compare दुई लिटर पानी / Dui litar paani / Two litres of water (The number counts litres, rather than bottles.)

## 5. Variations and agreement

Use verified spoken forms separately from punctuation and digit scripts. Familiarity with a written number does not establish that the learner can say it. Exact values can be used inside estimates, which G06-006 owns. Phase 2C owns systematic number vocabulary.

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

No difference in value follows merely from different comma placement. Have learners read with the key before attempting any spoken form.

**Example corrective retry.** Identify the unit before matching the numeral. Retry with an order that gives three litres. Use [variant-aware assessment](../proficiency-framework.md#variant-aware-assessment) for contextual mismatches and potentially valid but unreviewed alternatives.

## 9. Natural usage

**Draft conversational scenario.** Use a shop order for a small measure, then a budget discussion where the value is supplied and the display groups its digits differently. ## 10. Prerequisites

- **Required.** A quantity scene with the counted item or measured unit established.
- **Helpful.** Familiarity with the scenario vocabulary.
- **Co-taught.** Use the contrast in the guided example. Supply values, number words, clocks, labels or totals whenever they are supporting information.
- **Extension.** Refer to related canonical targets for independent evidence. Provide sufficient audio or scene cues for learners working without Devanagari.

### LO1 — Identify the quantity a cardinal expresses

- **Learner prompt.** In the water order, how many litres were requested? Supply the English gloss for the unit.
- **Expected response.** Two litres.
- **Assessment target.** LO1 only. Score applying quantity in a frame independently of memorised number words and classifier selection; establish arithmetic or scale facts with a key.
- **Feedback and retry.** Identify the unit before matching the numeral. Retry with an order that gives three litres.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, interpretation, scoring limits and acceptable contextual alternatives before use.

### LO2 — Distinguish numerical value from digit script or grouping

- **Learner prompt.** Compare १,००,०००, 1,00,000 and 100,000 using a supplied digit key. Do they denote the same value?
- **Expected response.** All are 100,000. Script or comma grouping differs.
- **Assessment target.** LO2 only. Score applying quantity in a frame independently of memorised number words and classifier selection; establish arithmetic or scale facts with a key.
- **Feedback and retry.** Remove the commas, match digits with the key, then compare १,००० with 1,000.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, interpretation, scoring limits and acceptable contextual alternatives before use.

### LO3 — Interpret lakh and crore in context

- **Learner prompt.** A budget label uses one crore. Using the displayed scale key, select 100,000 or 10,000,000.
- **Expected response.** 10,000,000.
- **Assessment target.** LO3 only. Score applying quantity in a frame independently of memorised number words and classifier selection; establish arithmetic or scale facts with a key.
- **Feedback and retry.** Match the budget term to the value on the key, then retry with one lakh.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, interpretation, scoring limits and acceptable contextual alternatives before use.

### LO4 — Produce an exact quantity in a supplied frame

- **Learner prompt.** The speaker wants exactly three litres. Given लिटर पानी / Litar paani / litres of water and the vocabulary card for three, give the quantity phrase.
- **Expected response.** तीन लिटर पानी / Tin litar paani / three litres of water.
- **Assessment target.** LO4 only. Score applying quantity in a frame independently of memorised number words and classifier selection; establish arithmetic or scale facts with a key.
- **Feedback and retry.** If you produced a bottle count or another value, hold the unit fixed and build the phrase for the requested count again.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, interpretation, scoring limits and acceptable contextual alternatives before use.
