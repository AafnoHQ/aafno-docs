## 3. Meaning and usage

Fractional quantities express a portion or a whole amount plus an additional part. The unit and scene establish what the fraction applies to. A mathematically equal expression may have a different distribution in speech.

## 4. Formation rules

Start with a supplied quartered unit. Match one or three parts to the quarter expressions. Join a reviewed fractional expression to a measurement unit, then compare one and a half with two and a half. Keep a separate clock display for clock-specific interpretations.

**Worked models.** आधा किलो चामल / Aadhaa kilo chaamal / Half a kilogram of rice (Recipe measurement; unit supplies the whole.) Compare एक चौथाइ / तीन चौथाइ / Ek chauthaai / Tin chauthaai / One quarter / three quarters (Select parts on the supplied visual.)

## 5. Variations and agreement

Collate lexical forms such as डेढ / Dedh / one and a half and अढाई / Adhaai / two and a half separately from expressions with सवा / Sawaa / quarter past or a quarter more, साढे / Saadhe / and a half, and पौने / Paune / a quarter short. Review their numeral limits, standalone readings and register. Treat exact value and a speaker’s estimating use separately.

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

A unit belongs to the whole, and the fraction specifies part of it. Ask for the unit before revisiting a mistaken value.

**Example corrective retry.** Mark the whole first, then shade three parts on a new bar. Use [variant-aware assessment](../proficiency-framework.md#variant-aware-assessment) for contextual mismatches and potentially valid but unreviewed alternatives.

### LO1 — Interpret a part of a supplied whole

- **Learner prompt.** Given a bar divided into four equal parts, point to the amount in the three-quarters expression.
- **Expected response.** Three of the four equal parts.

- **Feedback and retry.** Mark the whole first, then shade three parts on a new bar.

### LO2 — Identify a conventional mixed amount

- **Learner prompt.** Match the expression for one and a half kilograms to supplied diagrams for 1.5 and 2.5 kilograms.
- **Expected response.** The 1.5 kilogram diagram.
- **Assessment target.** LO2 only. Supply the values and fraction diagrams. Score the language interpretation or unit construction independently of computation and clock reading.

### LO3 — Combine a partial quantity with a supplied unit

- **Learner prompt.** Use the supplied half expression and kilogram vocabulary to request half a kilogram of rice with the model hidden.
- **Expected response.** आधा किलो चामल / Aadhaa kilo chaamal / half a kilogram of rice.
- **Assessment target.** LO3 only. Supply the values and fraction diagrams. Score the language interpretation or unit construction independently of computation and clock reading.

### LO4 — Distinguish exact fractional meaning from estimation

- **Learner prompt.** The recipe specifies half a kilogram; the shopper says ‘about half a kilogram’. Which specifies an exact target?
- **Expected response.** The recipe. The shopper presents the fraction as an estimate.
- **Assessment target.** LO4 only. Supply the values and fraction diagrams. Score the language interpretation or unit construction independently of computation and clock reading.
- **Feedback and retry.** Keep the fractional value fixed and identify what makes the second speaker less precise.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, interpretation, scoring limits and acceptable contextual alternatives before use.

### LO5 — Distinguish a general fractional quantity from a clock expression

- **Learner prompt.** Compare the mixed amount followed by ‘litres’ with the same fractional wording in a clock-reading scene. What does each refer to?
- **Expected response.** One measures volume, while the clock scene locates a time. G06-011 owns the clock reading.
- **Assessment target.** LO5 only. Supply the values and fraction diagrams. Score the language interpretation or unit construction independently of computation and clock reading.
- **Feedback and retry.** Ask what is being measured or scheduled, then retry in a kitchen or appointment scene.
