## 2. Learning outcomes

- LO1: Identify the intensity expressed qualitatively.
- LO2: Identify the modified quality or action.
- LO3: Distinguish degree from noun quantity and ambiguous extent.
- LO4: Produce a suitable degree expression.

## 3. Meaning and usage

Degree changes how strongly a quality or action is described. The same word can quantify books or suggest amount, duration or frequency with an activity. Determine its contribution from the target and context rather than an English gloss.

## 4. Formation rules

Hold the temperature quality fixed and compare the first three phrases. Ask what is intensified. Then move the modifier into a quantity phrase and an activity report, and compare which reading follows from the whole construction.

**Worked models.** अलि तातो / Ali taato / A little hot (Temperature described with limited intensity.) Compare धेरै तातो / Dherai taato / Very hot (A stronger description; contextual scale.) The examples are instructional candidates.

## 5. Variations and agreement

Intensity depends on context, register and combination; avoid fixed scales. G06 owns noun quantity, G18 comparison and detailed degree constructions, and G05-010 attachment and scope.

### Ambiguous extent

For the reading example, contrast a busy week with many reading sessions, a lengthy single session and a high number of pages. Ask which interpretation a reviewer finds natural with the same expression. The target is whether a learner distinguishes the relevant dimension from noun amount, not a claim that all three readings are available.

Test intensifiers in matched affirmative and negative contexts. Determine whether the speaker denies the quality altogether or only its strength. Keep proposed interactions with negation and other modifiers outside scoring until reviewed.

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

A learner can sound plausible while using a cue unrelated to the target. Test what they inferred with the two independent prompts in section 12.

**Corrective cue for the first prompt.** Bracket what follows the modifier in each and retry the interpretation.

**Corrective cue for the second prompt.** If the answer sounds stronger than requested, return to the temperature scenario and choose a lower-intensity expression.

Use the [variant-aware assessment policy](../proficiency-framework.md#variant-aware-assessment) if context permits more than one answer or the variant has not been reviewed.
