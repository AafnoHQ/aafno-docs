## 2. Learning outcomes

- LO1: Recognise a frequency cue.
- LO2: Interpret recurrence in affirmative and negative contexts.
- LO3: Distinguish frequency from time and habitual aspect.
- LO4: Produce a suitable frequency expression.

## 3. Meaning and usage

A frequency expression describes how often an event occurs over a relevant set of occasions. It narrows a routine report without forming the habitual verb or giving the date of one event.

## 4. Formation rules

Keep the football activity fixed and compare always, sometimes and the negative ever construction. Then use usually or rarely with an appropriate observation period. Interpret the last football example using the predicate’s negation as well.

**Worked models.** म सधैँ फुटबल खेल्छु। / Ma sadhaĩ football khelchhu. / I always play football. (Recurring occasions in the relevant context.) Compare म कहिलेकाहीँ फुटबल खेल्छु। / Ma kahilekaahĩ football khelchhu. / I sometimes play football. (Occurs on some occasions.) The examples are instructional candidates.

## 5. Variations and agreement

Frequency values are context-dependent. Avoid fixed proportions and a universal translation for कहिल्यै / Kahilyai / ever. G16 owns negation, and G09-001 owns the habitual construction.
