## 2. Learning outcomes

- LO1: Distinguish exact, estimated, ranged and unspecified quantities.
- LO2: Interpret an estimate without inventing bounds.
- LO3: Interpret a stated range.
- LO4: Produce an estimated amount using a reviewed frame.

## 3. Meaning and usage

An estimate gives a numerical anchor without committing to an exact value. A reported range supplies bounds in the intended context. ‘Many’ may express a sizeable amount without supplying a numerical anchor.

## 4. Formation rules

Start from an exact headcount. Add a candidate estimate marker before or after the count in reviewed patterns. Compare with a stated range and a nonnumeric answer. Keep the numeral and classifier supplied when testing the estimation marker.

**Worked models.** ठीक दस जना / Thik das jana / Exactly ten people (An attendance count, not an estimate.) Compare करिब दस जना / लगभग दस जना / Karib das jana / Lagbhag das jana / Approximately ten people (Two candidate pre-count estimate markers.)

## 5. Variations and agreement

Check register, placement, units and contextual range readings. No universal tolerance follows from about ten. जति / Jati / approximately in this frame can have different functions elsewhere. An endpoint range and an approximate pairing can be distinguished only in context.

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

If a learner assigns a hard tolerance to an estimate, have them identify any explicitly stated endpoint first.

**Example corrective retry.** Ask whether each report supplies one firm count, an anchor, bounds or no anchor; retry with a different headcount. Use [variant-aware assessment](../proficiency-framework.md#variant-aware-assessment) for contextual mismatches and potentially valid but unreviewed alternatives.

### LO1 — Distinguish exact, estimated, ranged and unspecified quantities

- **Learner prompt.** Sort the Nepali expressions in rows 1, 2, 4 and 5 into exact count, estimate, range and unspecified amount. Hide full glosses and provide the numeral key only.
- **Expected response.** Rows 1, 2, 4 and 5 respectively.
- **Assessment target.** LO1 only. Score exactness, anchor or stated bounds as appropriate. Supply numerals and intervals and leave classifier choice and numeric conversions outside the score.
- **Feedback and retry.** Ask whether each report supplies one firm count, an anchor, bounds or no anchor; retry with a different headcount.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, interpretation, scoring limits and acceptable contextual alternatives before use.

### LO2 — Interpret an estimate without inventing bounds

- **Learner prompt.** An organiser reports दस जना जति / Das jana jati. Supply a numeral key and the group context, with the full gloss hidden. Does the wording establish a count between nine and eleven?
- **Expected response.** No. Ten is the estimate’s anchor, and the phrase gives no universal bounds.
- **Assessment target.** LO2 only. Score exactness, anchor or stated bounds as appropriate. Supply numerals and intervals and leave classifier choice and numeric conversions outside the score.
- **Feedback and retry.** Check whether the wording actually states endpoints, then compare with a clearly bounded schedule estimate.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, interpretation, scoring limits and acceptable contextual alternatives before use.

### LO3 — Interpret a stated range

- **Learner prompt.** Read दस–बाह्र जना / Das-baahra jana as the organiser’s stated attendance range. With a numeral key and full gloss hidden, mark the endpoints.
- **Expected response.** Ten and twelve.
- **Assessment target.** LO3 only. Score exactness, anchor or stated bounds as appropriate. Supply numerals and intervals and leave classifier choice and numeric conversions outside the score.
- **Feedback and retry.** Point to both endpoints on a supplied number line, then retry with another supplied interval.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, interpretation, scoring limits and acceptable contextual alternatives before use.

### LO4 — Produce an estimated amount using a reviewed frame

- **Learner prompt.** Report ‘about ten people’ using a supplied ten-people phrase but without a model estimate.
- **Expected response.** करिब दस जना / Karib das jana / approximately ten people. Accept another reviewed option if appropriate.
- **Assessment target.** LO4 only. Score exactness, anchor or stated bounds as appropriate. Supply numerals and intervals and leave classifier choice and numeric conversions outside the score.
- **Feedback and retry.** If the result sounds exact, add an estimation cue and repeat the report for the same anchor.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, interpretation, scoring limits and acceptable contextual alternatives before use.
