- LO4: Contrast an intransitive frame with a transitive frame containing an omitted object.

## 3. Meaning and usage

In an intransitive use, the predicate does not require a direct object. It may still allow or select other expressions, such as a destination in a motion frame. Time, place and manner should not be counted automatically as direct objects.

## 4. Formation rules

Start with a sleep or cry report; identify who sleeps or cries. Compare the motion report and locate what answers where. Then listen to a short eating reply in a meal conversation and recover the food without counting visible nouns.

**Worked illustrations.** बच्चा सुत्छ। / Bachchaa sutchha. / The child sleeps. (Sleeper without a direct object.) Compare बच्चा रुन्छ। / Bachchaa runchha. / The child cries. (Participant undergoing or performing the event.)

## 5. Variations and agreement

Selected complement versus adjunct status may need separate evidence. A verb may occur in another construction with a different frame. Preserve those analyses under their respective owners.

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

**Diagnose the interpretation.** Identify the event first and retry with the cry report.

**Retry without leaking the target.** Track the recently introduced person and retry with another known participant. Use [variant-aware assessment](../proficiency-framework.md#variant-aware-assessment) when a plausible response depends on a reading or form still under review.

## 9. Natural usage

**Draft conversational scene.** A parent reports a child sleeping or crying, then a speaker explains where they are going. Neither report needs a direct object in its illustrated frame. Use the matching contextual utterances in section 6 and review their naturalness in the exchange.

### LO1 — Recognise a supported intransitive use

- **Learner prompt.** Read the sleep example without the full gloss. Does the reviewed predicate frame require a direct object?
- **Expected response.** No. The frame identifies a sleeper without requiring an entity slept upon.
- **Assessment target.** LO1 only. Assess direct-object requirements in the frame. G07-011 owns wider valency and complement analysis, G05 adverbial meaning and G14 marking.
- **Feedback and retry.** Identify the event first and retry with the cry report.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm the example, analysis and accepted responses before use.

### LO2 — Interpret participant requirements in a new clause

- **Learner prompt.** Read ऊ हाँस्छ। / U haanschha. with only the lexical gloss for laugh. Which participant is supplied in this frame, and is there a second participant that the verb needs as a direct object here?
- **Expected response.** The laughing participant is identified by ऊ / U and the situation. This draft frame does not require anyone or anything as a direct object.
- **Assessment target.** LO2 only. Assess direct-object requirements in the frame. G07-011 owns wider valency and complement analysis, G05 adverbial meaning and G14 marking.
- **Feedback and retry.** Track the recently introduced person and retry with another known participant.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm the example, analysis and accepted responses before use.

### LO3 — Distinguish a destination or circumstance from a direct object

- **Learner prompt.** In the motion report, what role does घर / Ghar / home play?
- **Expected response.** Destination of the movement, rather than a direct object.
- **Assessment target.** LO3 only. Assess direct-object requirements in the frame. G07-011 owns wider valency and complement analysis, G05 adverbial meaning and G14 marking.
- **Feedback and retry.** Ask where the speaker goes, then retry with a different destination.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm the example, analysis and accepted responses before use.

### LO4 — Contrast an intransitive frame with a transitive frame containing an omitted object

- **Learner prompt.** Compare the sleep report and the short eating reply after a plate of rice is offered. Why are the two frames different even though both lack a visible object?
- **Expected response.** Sleeping in this frame does not take a direct object. The meal context supplies the food for the eating frame.
- **Assessment target.** LO4 only. Assess direct-object requirements in the frame. G07-011 owns wider valency and complement analysis, G05 adverbial meaning and G14 marking.
- **Feedback and retry.** Recover the food in the second scene before deciding. Retry with a laugh reply and a short eating reply in new contexts.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm the example, analysis and accepted responses before use.
