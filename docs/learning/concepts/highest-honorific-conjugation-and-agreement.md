## 3. Meaning and usage

Illustrative register comparison:

तँ कहाँ जान्छस्?

Ta kahaã jaanchhas?

Where are you going?

तिमी कहाँ जान्छौ?

Timi kahaã jaanchhau?

Where are you going?

तपाईं कहाँ जानुहुन्छ?

Tapaai kahaã jaanuhunchha?

Where are you going?

हजुर कहाँ गइसिन्छ?

Hajur kahaã gaisinchha?

Where are you going?

These demonstrate different working honorific categories.

Their social appropriateness depends on context and validated usage.

Do not establish universal age-based, caste-based or community-based rules.

## 4. Formation rules

Existing research proposes a highest-honorific formation involving a verb stem and forms associated with बक्सिनु (Baksinu).

Illustrative draft:

खानु — Khaanu — To eat.

खाइ — Khai.

खाइबक्सिनु — Khaibaksinu.

खाइसिनु — Khaisinu.

The full and contracted forms require linguistic analysis.

जानु (Jaanu) has a proposed irregular गइ (Gai) pattern.

गइबक्सिनु — Gaibaksinu.

गइसिनु — Gaisinu.

Both mean "to go" in the relevant highest-honorific construction.

**Explicit product-owner clarification: Gaibaksinu and Gaisinu belong to the same honorific level and are used interchangeably within the product owner's family.**

Do not classify these as separate respect levels.

Do not describe the full form as inherently more respectful.

Do not describe the contracted form as inherently less respectful.

The family report establishes evidence of family usage, not universal distribution.

Do not mechanically generalise formation to every verb.

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

Address:

- Incorrect register.
- Mechanical overgeneralisation.
- Treating full and contracted forms as separate respect levels.
- Confusing imperatives with past forms.
- Incorrect negation.
- Incorrect agreement.
- Rejecting legitimate household alternatives.

Unknown household forms must not automatically be treated as errors.

**Feedback and retry.** Give the relationship and whether the speaker addresses or refers to the person. Ask learners to revisit a reviewed pattern for that context. When a full and contracted form correspond, keep the respect level fixed. Flag unfamiliar household alternatives for review.

## 9. Natural usage

Draft family dialogue:

हजुर सन्चै होइसिन्छ?

Hajur sanchai hoisinchha?

Are you well?

अँ, सन्चै छु।

Aã, sanchai chhu.

Yes, I am well.

हजुरले खाना खाइसियो?

Hajurle khaana khaisiyo?

Have you eaten? / Did you eat?

अँ, खाएँ।

Aã, khaaẽ.

Yes, I ate.

यहाँ बसिस्यो।

Yahaã basisyo.

Please sit here.

Validate naturalness, spelling, agreement, temporal interpretation and contextual register.

Investigate asymmetrical and reciprocal respectful usage.

## 10. Prerequisites

Verb meanings and address forms are required for productive tasks.

Basic conjugation is helpful.

Standard respectful conjugation is helpful but should not be imposed as an unnecessary barrier for heritage learners.

Tense, aspect, negation and modality depend on the task.

Social selection should be co-taught with G25 concepts.

Devanagari literacy is not required.
