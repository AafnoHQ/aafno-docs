# Reported information and hearsay

**Phase 2A.2 Step 3A specification draft.** Product-owner approval covers the pilot direction. Language examples and assessment alternatives await review. [Pilot index](index.md) · [Content QA](../content-qa.md).

## 1. Concept identity

- Catalogue ID: **G27-007** · [G27 — Evidentiality](../grammar-catalogue/honorifics-naturalness-and-advanced-language.md#g27).
- English title: Reported information and hearsay.
- Proposed Nepali terminology: श्रुत सूचना.
- Romanisation: Shruta suchanaa.
- Relevant particle: रे — Re.
- Concept type: Information-source marking.
- Catalogue learning outcome: Interpret and use hearsay रे to attribute information to another speaker or source, including in reported speech.
- Specification status: Draft implementing the approved pilot direction.
- Validation status: Linguistic and native-speaker review pending. Publication eligibility unestablished.
- CEFR assignment: Unassigned.
- Reconciled source: G23-016.

## 2. Learning outcomes

- LO1: Recognise spoken and written रे.
- LO2: Interpret its reported-information meaning.
- LO3: Distinguish reported information from source-unspecified assertions.
- LO4: Construct validated statements using रे.
- LO5: Relay information while preserving intended meaning and relevant references.
- LO6: Apply validated tense, aspect, polarity and honorific combinations.
- LO7: Distinguish information source from reliability and speaker certainty.
- LO8: Transfer the construction to unfamiliar information-relay contexts.

## 3. Meaning and usage

ऊ भोलि आउँछ।

U bholi aauchha.

He is coming tomorrow.

ऊ भोलि आउँछ रे।

U bholi aauchha re.

I hear he is coming tomorrow.

The second statement attributes information to another speaker or source.

Illustrate:

Original information source → current speaker → listener.

The original source need not be explicitly named.

Hearsay is a neutral information-source distinction.

Do not teach that रे necessarily means:

- False information.
- Gossip.
- Unreliable information.
- Doubt.
- Maybe.

Examples:

आज पानी पर्छ रे।

Aaja paani parchha re.

I hear it will rain today.

पसल बन्द छ रे।

Pasal banda chha re.

Apparently the shop is closed.

उनीहरूले जिते रे।

Uniharule jite re.

I hear they won.

दाइ नेपाल जानुभयो रे।

Daai Nepaal jaanubhayo re.

I hear older brother went to Nepal.

## 4. Formation rules

Illustrative basic construction:

Statement + रे.

Do not present this as an exhaustive particle-placement rule.

ऊ आयो रे।

U aayo re.

I hear he came.

ऊ आएन रे।

U aaena re.

I hear he did not come.

ऊ आउँछ रे।

U aauchha re.

I hear he will come.

Reported speech transformation:

Ram originally says:

म भोलि आउँछु।

Ma bholi aauchhu.

I will come tomorrow.

A speaker relays this information on the same day:

राम भोलि आउँछ रे।

Raam bholi aauchha re.

Ram says he is coming tomorrow.

Personal reference, temporal reference and agreement must be adapted according to the reporting context.

Do not impose automatic tense changes.

Detailed reporting transformations belong to the relevant G23 concepts.

G27-007 owns the reported-information meaning.

Honorific examples:

ऊ आयो रे।

U aayo re.

I hear he came.

उनी आए रे।

Uni aae re.

I hear he/she came, with the relevant agreement requiring review.

उहाँ आउनुभयो रे।

Uhã aaunubhayo re.

I hear he/she came, respectfully.

Investigate both highest-honorific variants where applicable.

Do not invent a highest-honorific hearsay paradigm.

Question and response research candidates:

हो रे?

Ho re?

Is that what they say?

के रे?

Ke re?

What did they say? / What was that?

के भन्यो रे?

Ke bhanyo re?

What did they reportedly say?

Investigate intonation, clarification, repetition and discourse functions.

## 7. Related constructions and contrasts

Four-way illustrative comparison:

ऊ घरमा छ।

U gharmaa chha.

He is at home.

ऊ घरमा छ रे।

U gharmaa chha re.

I hear he is at home.

ऊ घरमा रहेछ।

U gharmaa rahechha.

It turns out he is at home.

ऊ घरमा होला।

U gharmaa holaa.

He is probably at home.

Distinguish:

- Source-unspecified assertion.
- Reported information.
- Discovery.
- Conjecture.

English "apparently" can be ambiguous and should not be used as the sole explanation.

An unmarked assertion does not automatically establish firsthand knowledge.

Information source and speaker certainty are independent dimensions.

A speaker may have high or low confidence in information obtained from another person.

Do not encode certainty solely through the presence of रे.

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

Address:

- Interpreting रे as "maybe".
- Assuming reported information is false.
- Treating unmarked assertions as necessarily firsthand.
- Confusing reported information with discovery.
- Unnecessary tense changes.
- Incorrect personal or temporal reference.
- Incorrect honorific agreement.
- Assuming every occurrence of रे has an identical discourse function.

Example interpretation task:

आज पसल बन्द छ रे।

Aaja pasal banda chha re.

I hear the shop is closed today.

The learner should identify reported information, not necessarily false or firsthand information.

**Feedback and retry.** Ask how the speaker learned the information and whether they expressed any certainty. Let the learner retell it to a new listener after identifying which references need updating. Treat an explicit reporting verb as possible communicative success and assess रे separately.

## 9. Natural usage

दाइ कहिले आउनुहुन्छ?

Daai kahile aaunuhunchha?

When is older brother coming?

भोलि आउनुहुन्छ रे।

Bholi aaunuhunchha re.

I hear he is coming tomorrow.

कति बजे?

Kati baje?

What time?

दस बजे आइपुग्नुहुन्छ रे।

Das baje aaipugnuhunchha re.

I hear he will arrive at ten.

कसले भन्यो?

Kasle bhanyo?

Who said that?

आमाले भन्नुभयो।

Aamaale bhannubhayo.

Mother said.

This integrates:

- Subject omission.
- Honorific agreement.
- Agentive marking.
- Reported information.
- Question formation.

Investigate short responses such as:

हो रे — Ho re — Reportedly yes.

होइन रे — Hoina re — Reportedly no.

## 10. Prerequisites

Sentence components are helpful.

Pronouns are required for relevant reporting transformations.

Conjugation may be co-taught.

Tense, aspect, polarity and register depend on the task.

More complex reported-speech transformations should be co-taught with G23 concepts.

Technical terminology and Devanagari literacy are not prerequisites for oral learning.

## 12. Activity types

Introduce information relay as a reusable activity pattern.

Illustrative tasks:

1. A friend says Ram is coming tomorrow. Relay the information.
2. A relative says the shop is closed. Tell another person.
3. Ram speaks in the first person. Relay his statement in the appropriate third-person form on the same day.
4. Hear a statement about a football team winning. Identify whether रे establishes source, truth or firsthand knowledge.

Include:

- Listening.
- Meaning contrasts.
- Sentence transformation.
- Personal reference.
- Temporal reference.
- Agreement correction.
- Spoken roleplay.

English glosses may be revealed after an attempt where displaying them beforehand would invalidate the assessed outcome.

- Source distinction.
- Construction.
- Information preservation.

Independent task:

Relay family news involving:

- A relative arriving tomorrow.
- A shop being closed.
- A football team winning yesterday.
- Changed travel plans.

Do not explicitly prompt the learner to use रे during the independent target-selection task.

An explicit reporting verb may demonstrate successful communication but does not independently establish mastery of रे.
