## 2. Learning outcomes

- LO2: Distinguish a dictionary form from a form used in a clause.
- LO3: Link encountered forms to a dictionary entry.
- LO4: Recover the entry from a multiword predicate.

## 3. Meaning and usage

A dictionary form is a lookup form for a verb. It helps connect the different shapes encountered in speech and text to a lexical meaning. A familiar ending is useful evidence in reviewed examples; it does not tell you the syntactic function of every occurrence.

## 4. Formation rules

Start with the play family. Compare the entry, a single habitual form, a past form and the multiword predicate. For lookup, isolate the lexical part of the last example rather than searching the whole predicate as if it were one word. Repeat the comparison with the eat family.

**Worked illustrations.** खेल्नु / Khelnu / To play (Dictionary entry.) Compare खेल्छु / Khelchhu / I play (Encountered habitual form.)

**Diagnose the interpretation.** Compare the entry marker in the reviewed pair; retry with the eat pair.

**Retry without leaking the target.** Compare what the speaker says with what a lookup entry lists, then classify a new past reply. Use [variant-aware assessment](../proficiency-framework.md#variant-aware-assessment) when a plausible response depends on a reading or form still under review.

## 9. Natural usage

**Draft conversational scene.** In a library, a learner hears the ongoing play report and uses its lexical element to look up the verb. Compare the entry with a short past report from a different speaker. Use the matching contextual utterances in section 6 and review their naturalness in the exchange.

## 12. Activity types

Row references are to section 6. Provide Devanagari, Romanisation and English by default. For scored work, hide any model answer, full gloss, row label or annotation that discloses the target. Keep the Nepali forms that learners must interpret or recognise visible. Supply lexical meanings without revealing the target form relationship or grammatical interpretation.

### LO1 — Recognise a dictionary-form verb

- **Learner prompt.** With glosses for lexical meaning only, select the dictionary entry from खेल्नु / Khelnu and खेल्छु / Khelchhu.
- **Expected response.** खेल्नु / Khelnu / to play.
- **Assessment target.** LO1 only. Assess form and entry relationships. Phase 2C owns systematic verb vocabulary, G07-002 stem analysis, and G20 specific non-finite uses.
- **Feedback and retry.** Compare the entry marker in the reviewed pair; retry with the eat pair.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm the example, analysis and accepted responses before use.
