## 2. Learning outcomes

- LO1: Identify numeral, classifier and noun components.
- LO2: Assemble a supported phrase from supplied components.
- LO3: Integrate supplied modifiers in a reviewed phrase.
- LO4: Recover omitted elements from context without assuming unrestricted omission.

## 3. Meaning and usage

Numeral, classifier and noun contribute different information to the whole phrase. Compose the order while holding number value and classifier choice fixed. Add a demonstrative or adjective only in a supported frame.

## 4. Formation rules

Start with supplied three, counting form and book components. Join the numeral and counting form in the illustrated spelling before the noun. Contrast the separately written human count; then place an already selected demonstrative and adjective around an object count.

**Worked models.** तीनवटा किताब / Tinwata kitaab / Three books (Numeral–general classifier–noun.) Compare दुई जना विद्यार्थी / Dui jana vidyaarthi / Two students (Separately written human classifier frame.)

## 5. Variations and agreement

Review ordering, classifier omission and noun recovery separately. Surface plurality or an adjective form can be supplied for this task. Quantifier combinations need contextual evidence. A grammatical component alone does not license every possible order.

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

An appropriate classifier choice can still be placed incorrectly. Keep the classifier supplied and focus the retry on ordering.

**Example corrective retry.** Mark the numeral first, then separate the counting element from the thing counted on a new phrase. Use [variant-aware assessment](../proficiency-framework.md#variant-aware-assessment) for contextual mismatches and potentially valid but unreviewed alternatives.

## 9. Natural usage

**Draft conversational scenario.** Use a shop list with a counted item and a particular set described by a demonstrative and adjective. In a follow-up, recover the item from an abbreviated count. Use the appropriate contextual models from section 6 for the response. Review naturalness and pronunciation before using this exchange as learner-facing material.

## 10. Prerequisites

- **Required.** All relevant components supplied with an intended referent.
- **Helpful.** Familiarity with the scenario vocabulary.
- **Co-taught.** Use the contrast in the guided example. Supply values, number words, clocks, labels or totals whenever they are supporting information.
- **Extension.** Refer to related canonical targets for independent evidence. Provide sufficient audio or scene cues for learners working without Devanagari.

### LO1 — Identify numeral, classifier and noun components

- **Learner prompt.** Identify the numeral, classifier and noun in the human phrase.
- **Expected response.** दुई / Dui / two, जना / Jana / human counting classifier, विद्यार्थी / Vidyaarthi / student.
- **Assessment target.** LO1 only. Score the assembly of the supplied components. Keep classifier choice, noun plurality, adjective agreement and stand-alone demonstrative choice independent.
- **Feedback and retry.** Mark the numeral first, then separate the counting element from the thing counted on a new phrase.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, interpretation, scoring limits and acceptable contextual alternatives before use.

### LO2 — Assemble a supported phrase from supplied components

- **Learner prompt.** Arrange supplied cards for three, general classifier and book. Write or say the assembled Nepali phrase without a model.
- **Expected response.** तीनवटा किताब / Tinwata kitaab / three books.
- **Assessment target.** LO2 only. Score the assembly of the supplied components. Keep classifier choice, noun plurality, adjective agreement and stand-alone demonstrative choice independent.
- **Feedback and retry.** If the host noun interrupts the counting expression, move the numeral and classifier together and retry with the apple noun supplied.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, interpretation, scoring limits and acceptable contextual alternatives before use.

### LO3 — Integrate supplied modifiers in a reviewed phrase

- **Learner prompt.** Arrange the supplied demonstrative, count, adjective and book noun to refer to the previously indicated three new books.
- **Expected response.** ती तीनवटा नयाँ किताब / Ti tinwata nayaã kitaab / those three new books, pending validation.
- **Assessment target.** LO3 only. Score the assembly of the supplied components. Keep classifier choice, noun plurality, adjective agreement and stand-alone demonstrative choice independent.
- **Feedback and retry.** Locate the entity being identified, then assemble its count and description. On retry keep the supplied agreement form unchanged.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, interpretation, scoring limits and acceptable contextual alternatives before use.

### LO4 — Recover omitted elements from context without assuming unrestricted omission

- **Learner prompt.** A bookseller just asked how many books you want. Read तीनवटा / Tinwata with its full gloss hidden. What does this reply leave unsaid?
- **Expected response.** The noun ‘books’ is understood; the count and classifier remain explicit.
- **Assessment target.** LO4 only. Score the assembly of the supplied components. Keep classifier choice, noun plurality, adjective agreement and stand-alone demonstrative choice independent.
- **Feedback and retry.** Return to the question to recover the noun. Retry after a new count question where a different item is salient.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, interpretation, scoring limits and acceptable contextual alternatives before use.
