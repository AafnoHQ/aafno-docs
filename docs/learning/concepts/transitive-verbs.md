## 3. Meaning and usage

Transitivity concerns the capacity of a construction to link the verb to a direct object. A noun being semantically affected is not a sufficient diagnostic, and the direct object need not be overt everywhere. Context can establish an understood referent.

## 4. Formation rules

Compare reading a book, eating rice and a short eating reply after lunch is already under discussion. Ask what the verb relates to, then add a time or destination foil. A prior turn can recover an object without supplying an overt noun in the reply.

**Worked illustrations.** म किताब पढ्छु। / Ma kitaab padhchhu. / I read a book. (Book is the entity read.) Compare म भात खान्छु। / Ma bhaat khaanchhu. / I eat rice. (Rice is the food in the frame.)

## 5. Variations and agreement

Review when an unexpressed object has a particular referent and when general activity is the better interpretation. Compare frames by use; do not derive a universal case or agreement rule here.

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

**Diagnose the interpretation.** Use the predicate frame rather than counting nouns. Retry with the eating and going pair.

**Retry without leaking the target.** Ask what was written rather than who wrote it. Retry with the reading predicate and its object. Use [variant-aware assessment](../proficiency-framework.md#variant-aware-assessment) when a plausible response depends on a reading or form still under review.

## 9. Natural usage

**Draft conversational scene.** A host offers rice and hears the short eating reply. Compare it with a report about reading a book and with a motion report that supplies a destination. Use the matching contextual utterances in section 6 and review their naturalness in the exchange.

### LO1 — Recognise a supported transitive construction in context

- **Learner prompt.** Compare म किताब पढ्छु। / Ma kitaab padhchhu. and म घर जान्छु। / Ma ghar jaanchhu. with only lexical glosses supplied. Which construction relates its verb to a direct object in the reviewed analysis?
- **Expected response.** The reading example. The book is what is read.
- **Assessment target.** LO1 only. Assess the relation between predicate and direct object in context. Component labeling, detailed case and object agreement remain G01 and G08/G14 evidence.
- **Feedback and retry.** Use the predicate frame rather than counting nouns. Retry with the eating and going pair.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm the example, analysis and accepted responses before use.

### LO2 — Interpret the direct-object relation in an encountered clause

- **Learner prompt.** Read उसले चिठी लेख्यो। / Usle chithi lekhyo. Which entity is produced by the action?
- **Expected response.** चिठी / Chithi / letter.
- **Assessment target.** LO2 only. Assess the relation between predicate and direct object in context. Component labeling, detailed case and object agreement remain G01 and G08/G14 evidence.
- **Feedback and retry.** Ask what was written rather than who wrote it. Retry with the reading predicate and its object.

### LO3 — Recover a contextually omitted object

- **Learner prompt.** Rice has just been offered. Read the short reply in row 3 without a full gloss. What food can the listener recover in this scene?
- **Expected response.** The offered rice. It is understood, with no overt object in the reply.
- **Assessment target.** LO3 only. Assess the relation between predicate and direct object in context. Component labeling, detailed case and object agreement remain G01 and G08/G14 evidence.
- **Feedback and retry.** Return to the offer and retry after a different food is introduced. Avoid inferring a particular food when the context is absent.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm the example, analysis and accepted responses before use.

### LO4 — Distinguish a direct object from an added circumstance

- **Learner prompt.** Compare किताब / Kitaab / book in the read frame with घर / Ghar / home in the go frame. Which is a direct object and which is a destination?
- **Expected response.** Book is the entity read and home is the destination.
- **Assessment target.** LO4 only. Assess the relation between predicate and direct object in context. Component labeling, detailed case and object agreement remain G01 and G08/G14 evidence.
- **Feedback and retry.** Ask what the participant reads versus where the participant goes, then retry with a different destination.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm the example, analysis and accepted responses before use.
