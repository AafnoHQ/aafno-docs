## 3. Meaning and usage

Several verbal elements can work together in a predicate. The main lexical element contributes the activity and a supporting form can help express grammatical meaning. A second verb is not necessarily an auxiliary.

## 4. Formation rules

Compare the habitual reading report with the ongoing reading report. In the latter, the reading element contributes lexical activity and the auxiliary participates in expressing the ongoing predicate. Compare the perfect construction and a sequence that names two actions.

**Worked illustrations.** म पढ्छु। / Ma padhchhu. / I read. (Single inflected lexical verb.) Compare म पढ्दै छु। / Ma padhdai chhu. / I am reading. (Lexical verbal element and supporting auxiliary in a reviewed-draft progressive.)

## 5. Variations and agreement

Classify forms by their use in a construction, not by a fixed list. A form related to हुनु / Hunu / to be may also have copular or existential functions. Compound, light-verb and serial analyses require contextual evidence. Relevant validated register comparisons may eventually extend this work. [G08-005](highest-honorific-conjugation-and-agreement.md) owns highest-honorific conjugation and agreement. G12-004, G25-005 and G31-007 retain imperative formation, social selection and family usage. Do not derive family forms from standard respectful forms.

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

**Diagnose the interpretation.** Contrast the same auxiliary in two activities and retry by pointing to the activity-bearing element.

**Retry without leaking the target.** Look for the element that works with the lexical form rather than naming another action. Try the perfect example. Use [variant-aware assessment](../proficiency-framework.md#variant-aware-assessment) when a plausible response depends on a reading or form still under review.

## 9. Natural usage

**Draft conversational scene.** A friend asks what someone is doing and hears the ongoing reading report. Later the same speaker describes eating and then going home, allowing comparison of one lexical activity with two. Use the matching contextual utterances in section 6 and review their naturalness in the exchange.

## 12. Activity types

Row references are to section 6. Provide Devanagari, Romanisation and English by default. For scored work, hide any model answer, full gloss, row label or annotation that discloses the target. ### LO1 — Identify the main lexical element

- **Learner prompt.** Which element in the ongoing reading report contributes the activity?

- **Assessment target.** LO1 only. Assess how the verbal parts work together. G01-002 owns independent predicate-span identification, G08-011 detailed auxiliary agreement and G09 specific aspect constructions. G13 owns complex verbal construction classifications.

### LO3 — Explain the lexical contribution

- **Learner prompt.** What activity does the lexical element contribute in the first and second reports?
- **Expected response.** Reading or studying in both, as supported by the scene.
- **Assessment target.** LO3 only. Assess how the verbal parts work together. G01-002 owns independent predicate-span identification, G08-011 detailed auxiliary agreement and G09 specific aspect constructions. G13 owns complex verbal construction classifications.
- **Feedback and retry.** Keep the activity fixed while comparing the constructions; retry with a listening example.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm the example, analysis and accepted responses before use.

### LO4 — Explain the grammatical contribution in context

- **Learner prompt.** With only the lexical meaning supplied, compare म पढ्छु। / Ma padhchhu. in a routine conversation and म पढ्दै छु। / Ma padhdai chhu. in a reply about the present moment. What grammatical contribution does the second construction make when the activity stays the same?
- **Expected response.** It presents reading as in progress in the supplied scene. The grammatical meaning comes from the construction.
- **Assessment target.** LO4 only. Assess how the verbal parts work together. G01-002 owns independent predicate-span identification, G08-011 detailed auxiliary agreement and G09 specific aspect constructions. G13 owns complex verbal construction classifications.
- **Feedback and retry.** Hold the activity constant and compare a routine with what the speaker is doing now. Retry with a fresh scene.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm the example, analysis and accepted responses before use.

### LO5 — Identify the complete construction without awarding separate predicate-span mastery

- **Learner prompt.** From the ongoing Nepali report, select the verbal elements that work together to express the activity and its grammatical presentation.
- **Expected response.** पढ्दै छु / Padhdai chhu / am reading. Both elements work together.
- **Assessment target.** LO5 only. Assess how the verbal parts work together. G01-002 owns independent predicate-span identification, G08-011 detailed auxiliary agreement and G09 specific aspect constructions. G13 owns complex verbal construction classifications.
- **Feedback and retry.** If you selected only one, pair the lexical activity with its auxiliary and retry with the perfect candidate.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm the example, analysis and accepted responses before use.

### LO6 — Distinguish an auxiliary pattern from a second lexical action

- **Learner prompt.** Compare म पढ्दै छु। / Ma padhdai chhu. and खाना खाएर घर जान्छु। / Khaanaa khaaera ghar jaanchhu. in a routine conversation. Supply lexical meanings only. Which report contains a second lexical action rather than a supporting auxiliary for one action?
- **Expected response.** The eating-then-going report names eating and going. The reading report expresses one lexical activity with auxiliary support.
- **Assessment target.** LO6 only. Assess how the verbal parts work together. G01-002 owns independent predicate-span identification, G08-011 detailed auxiliary agreement and G09 specific aspect constructions. G13 owns complex verbal construction classifications.
- **Feedback and retry.** Count the distinct activities and retry with a copular or multi-action foil.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm the example, analysis and accepted responses before use.
