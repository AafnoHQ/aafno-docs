## 3. Meaning and usage

Relative reference identifies someone or something through a description. The contest rule links the first arrival and the prize recipient without naming that person.

## 4. Formation rules

Link जो / Jo / whoever to उसले / Usle / that person as agent in the rule. In the book promise, first identify the selected book, then track it as the object of purchase. Contrast a question that requests a choice.

**Worked models.** जो पहिले आउँछ, उसले पुरस्कार पाउँछ। / Jo pahile aauchha, usle puraskaar paauchha. / Whoever comes first receives a prize. Compare जुन किताब तिमीले छान्छौ, त्यो म किन्छु। / Jun kitaab timile chhaanchhau, tyo ma kinchhu. / I'll buy whichever book you choose. Use the situations in section 6 to establish the intended readings.

## 5. Variations and agreement

Corresponding expressions and cases depend on the construction. A stand-alone form and a noun-modifying form illustrate options; neither example creates a universal template or person/entity rule. G22 owns the detailed clause patterns.

### Reference check

Number the participants or objects before linking related expressions. In the book promise, let two books remain available and note that the bought book is constrained by the listener's eventual choice, even before its identity is known. In the paired question the listener is being asked to reveal that choice. A recognition task may ask learners to locate जो / Jo / whoever or जुन / Jun / whichever without scoring whether they linked the right entity.

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

**Likely misunderstanding.** A learner may treat the relative expression as a question or assume that its counterpart refers to someone else.

**Retry.** Draw one line rather than assigning a different participant to each clause. For the contrast target, try replying with a book name and see which utterance is asking for that information. Use the [variant-aware assessment policy](../proficiency-framework.md#variant-aware-assessment) for contextual mismatches and unreviewed forms.
