## 2. Learning outcomes

- LO4: Distinguish omitted nouns from omitted classifiers using contextual evidence.

## 3. Meaning and usage

The classifier जना / Jana / human counting classifier can appear with a numeral when counting people, with a noun stated or recoverable. A person count and a grammatical claim about classifier obligatoriness are different.

## 4. Formation rules

Count people in an attendance scene with the numeral supplied. Identify the counting form and noun, then compare a follow-up that leaves the type of people unstated. Check what the previous question makes recoverable.

**Worked models.** एक जना / दुई जना / तीन जना / Ek jana / Dui jana / Tin jana / One person / two people / three people (People counted, noun supplied by context.) Compare तीन जना विद्यार्थी / Tin jana vidyaarthi / Three students (Explicit human noun.)

## 5. Variations and agreement

Investigate noun or classifier omission separately, along with alternatives and their social or register implications. Do not assume all numerical references to people require the classifier or that alternatives are intrinsically inappropriate.

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

A short count can still contain the classifier. Help the learner recover the omitted person noun from the prior turn.

**Example corrective retry.** Separate the numeral, classifier and noun, then locate the classifier in the people phrase. Use [variant-aware assessment](../proficiency-framework.md#variant-aware-assessment) for contextual mismatches and potentially valid but unreviewed alternatives.

## 9. Natural usage

**Draft conversational scenario.** A teacher asks about students and receives a short people-count reply. Repeat after a question about volunteers to show why context changes the recovered noun. Use the appropriate contextual models from section 6 for the response. Review naturalness and pronunciation before using this exchange as learner-facing material.

## 10. Prerequisites

- **Required.** A context specifying the group of people and a supplied numeral.
- **Helpful.** Familiarity with the scenario vocabulary.
- **Co-taught.** Use the contrast in the guided example. Supply values, number words, clocks, labels or totals whenever they are supporting information.
- **Extension.** Refer to related canonical targets for independent evidence. Provide sufficient audio or scene cues for learners working without Devanagari.
