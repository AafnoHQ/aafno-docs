## 3. Meaning and usage

A demonstrative determiner helps identify the referent of a noun. The same surface form can stand alone in another construction. Physical distance is a useful scene cue, while prior mention and shared attention can also matter.

## 4. Formation rules

Compare a reply pointing to an object without naming it and a reply naming it as a book. In the second sentence, bracket the demonstrative and noun together. Compare single and plural examples and then add a reviewed count phrase in an identifiable scene.

**Worked models.** यो राम्रो छ। / Yo raamro chha. / This is good. (Standalone demonstrative; pronoun comparison.) Compare यो किताब राम्रो छ। / Yo kitaab raamro chha. / This book is good. (Demonstrative modifies the book noun.)

## 5. Variations and agreement

Treat proximity, number and contextual choice as working contrasts. Review what happens with independently established plurality or explicit suffixes. Pronoun reference, noun number, word order and broader definiteness have separate owners.

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

A standalone expression can make sense in dialogue without demonstrating this noun-modifying construction. Give the noun for a fresh attempt.

**Example corrective retry.** Mark the stated noun, then retry with a pair that has the same demonstrative but changes whether a noun is present. Use [variant-aware assessment](../proficiency-framework.md#variant-aware-assessment) for contextual mismatches and potentially valid but unreviewed alternatives.

## 9. Natural usage

**Draft conversational scenario.** At a stall, identify a visible individual book. Then arrange to buy a previously discussed set out of sight, testing what the listener can recover. Use the appropriate contextual models from section 6 for the response. Review naturalness and pronunciation before using this exchange as learner-facing material.

## 10. Prerequisites

- **Required.** A stated noun and shared attention or sufficient discourse context.
- **Helpful.** Familiarity with the scenario vocabulary.
- **Co-taught.** Use the contrast in the guided example. Supply values, number words, clocks, labels or totals whenever they are supporting information.
- **Extension.** Refer to related canonical targets for independent evidence. Provide sufficient audio or scene cues for learners working without Devanagari.

### LO1 — Distinguish noun modification from standalone reference

- **Learner prompt.** Which of the first two uses places the demonstrative inside a noun phrase with a stated noun?
- **Expected response.** Row 2.
- **Assessment target.** LO1 only. Score use of the demonstrative to modify a stated noun. G02-005 owns standalone reference and G03-008 owns definiteness and specificity.
- **Feedback and retry.** Mark the stated noun, then retry with a pair that has the same demonstrative but changes whether a noun is present.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, interpretation, scoring limits and acceptable contextual alternatives before use.

### LO2 — Identify the modified noun

- **Learner prompt.** What noun is being modified in row 2?
- **Expected response.** किताब / Kitaab / book.
- **Assessment target.** LO2 only. Score use of the demonstrative to modify a stated noun. G02-005 owns standalone reference and G03-008 owns definiteness and specificity.
- **Feedback and retry.** Separate the property assertion from the noun phrase, then locate the noun again.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, interpretation, scoring limits and acceptable contextual alternatives before use.

### LO3 — Interpret visible and discourse referents and relevant number

- **Learner prompt.** The speakers have just discussed the books on one shelf but cannot see that shelf. Would a plural demonstrative phrase identify one book or a set in that dialogue, and what makes the referent recoverable?
- **Expected response.** The previously discussed books if the set is recoverable. No fixed physical distance follows from the wording alone.
- **Assessment target.** LO3 only. Score use of the demonstrative to modify a stated noun. G02-005 owns standalone reference and G03-008 owns definiteness and specificity.
- **Feedback and retry.** Ask what the previous turn established and whether one book or a set is meant; retry with a different salient set.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, interpretation, scoring limits and acceptable contextual alternatives before use.

### LO4 — Produce a reviewed demonstrative noun phrase

- **Learner prompt.** Point to an individual book in your hands and give its Nepali noun phrase with a demonstrative. Supply the noun, but hide the model phrase.
- **Expected response.** यो किताब / Yo kitaab / this book, in the supplied scene.
- **Assessment target.** LO4 only. Score use of the demonstrative to modify a stated noun. G02-005 owns standalone reference and G03-008 owns definiteness and specificity.
- **Feedback and retry.** If the noun is omitted, the listener may still identify the book, but retry using a demonstrative that modifies the supplied noun.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, interpretation, scoring limits and acceptable contextual alternatives before use.
