## 2. Learning outcomes

- LO1: Interpret an ordinary clock expression.

- LO5: Produce a numerical duration phrase.

## 3. Meaning and usage

A clock reading locates an event, while a duration gives how long it lasts. Since or until marks a boundary. Fractional clock wording uses conventional relations to a clock hour and should be taught in that setting.

## 4. Formation rules

Place five o’clock on a timeline. Match quarter past, half past and quarter to separately to displayed clocks. Then compare the event marker with a five-hour span and with the start and end boundary phrases. A start alone does not tell the elapsed amount.

**Worked models.** पाँच बजे / Paanch baje / At five o’clock (Appointment time. Morning or evening requires context.) Compare सवा पाँच / Sawaa paanch / Quarter past five, 5:15 (Conventional fractional clock candidate.)

## 5. Variations and agreement

Use supplied clock values and minute readings. Elicit whether बजे / Baje / o’clock is spoken or omitted, and compare digital readings and parts of the day. Neither the hour alone nor a duration establishes morning versus evening or verb tense.

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

Have the learner point to a clock position or draw a span before correcting the spoken expression. Clock and duration errors have different feedback.

**Example corrective retry.** Use the supplied number key to match the named hour, then try a new clock point. Use [variant-aware assessment](../proficiency-framework.md#variant-aware-assessment) for contextual mismatches and potentially valid but unreviewed alternatives.

## 9. Natural usage

**Draft conversational scenario.** Make an appointment at a clock point, describe a train wait by duration and distinguish the latest arrival boundary from that duration. Use the appropriate contextual models from section 6 for the response. Review naturalness and pronunciation before using this exchange as learner-facing material.

- **Helpful.** Familiarity with the scenario vocabulary.

- **Extension.** Refer to related canonical targets for independent evidence. Provide sufficient audio or scene cues for learners working without Devanagari.

### LO4 — Distinguish time of occurrence from duration

- **Learner prompt.** Compare पाँच बजे / Paanch baje and पाँच घण्टा / Paanch ghantaa in a travel scene, with the full glosses hidden. Which locates an appointment on the clock and which can give the length of a wait?
- **Expected response.** At five gives the clock point; five hours gives a duration without a start time.
- **Assessment target.** LO4 only. Score linguistic readings with displayed clock values and durations. Keep arithmetic, independent clock-reading skill, tense and detailed boundary morphology separate.
