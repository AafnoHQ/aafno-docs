# First-person pronouns

**Phase 2A.2 Step 3B Batch 1 specification draft.** [Batch register](../specification-batches.md#step-3b-batch-1) · [Content QA](../content-qa.md).

- English title: First-person pronouns.

- Concept type: Speaker reference and group membership.
- Catalogue learning outcome: Use म and हामी to refer to oneself or a group that includes oneself.
- Specification status: Draft.

- CEFR assignment: Unassigned.

## 2. Learning outcomes

- LO1: Recognise म in speech and writing.
- LO2: Recognise हामी in speech and writing.
- LO3: Distinguish the individual speaker from a group that includes the speaker.
- LO4: Infer possible listener inclusion or exclusion from context.
- LO5: Select an appropriate first-person reference in a familiar frame.
- LO6: Use and interpret first-person reference across changes of speaker.

## 3. Meaning and usage

म / Ma / I refers to the current speaker. हामी / Haami / we includes the current speaker in a group. Context specifies who else belongs to that group. A listener may or may not be included.

## 4. Formation rules

Track speech turns and ask who is speaking. Select the individual or group form for the intended referent, then check the associated predicate separately. Introduce case and possessive forms through their owners when context requires them, and recover omitted first-person subjects using G01-004.

## 5. Variations and agreement

Compare explicit and understood reference, group membership and agreement. A different speaker changes the person picked out by म. A stable group can still be described differently by different speakers. Review plural variants, case and possessive relationships.

## 6. Contextual examples

The examples below are draft instructional illustrations. Context and analysis describe the intended readings for review. Source is the Batch 1 brief or an illustrative authoring example. Audio and reuse evidence are pending. The canonical assessment target is G02-001.

| Devanagari | Romanisation | English | Context and analysis to review |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| म फुटबल खेल्छु। | Ma football khelchhu. | I play football. | Describe the speaker’s own sports routine. |
| हामी फुटबल खेल्छौँ। | Haami football khelchhaũ. | We play football. | Speak for a team that includes the speaker; ask whether the listener belongs to it. |
| हामी भोलि जान्छौँ। | Haami bholi jaanchhaũ. | We will go tomorrow. | Use two scenarios: an invitation including the listener and a team update excluding them. |

## 7. Related constructions and contrasts

Pronoun selection is evidence of speaker reference. Agreement, possession, case and omission are separate targets even when used in the same turn.

Related canonical targets: [G01-004](../grammar-catalogue/grammar-foundations.md#g01), [G02-004](../grammar-catalogue/grammar-foundations.md#g02), [G08](../grammar-catalogue/verbs-and-conjugation.md#g08), [G14](../grammar-catalogue/relationships-and-transformations.md#g14), [G15](../grammar-catalogue/relationships-and-transformations.md#g15).

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

If a learner always includes the listener in हामी, ask who is going in this scenario. Replay with a different speaker or group and have the learner point to the speaker first. For an ending mismatch, give agreement feedback separately.

Use the [variant-aware assessment policy](../proficiency-framework.md#variant-aware-assessment) for reviewed alternatives, contextual mismatches and unreviewed forms.

## 9. Natural usage

Have partners alternate a self-description with a group plan. Use the same we sentence in an invitation and in a report about a group that excludes the addressee.

Draft exchange for review. Specify the respondent's team in the scenario and vary whether the addressee belongs to it.

| Turn | Devanagari | Romanisation | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Question | तिमी फुटबल खेल्छौ? | Timi football khelchhau? | Do you play football? |
| Reply | हामी फुटबल खेल्छौँ। | Haami football khelchhaũ. | We play football. |

## 10. Prerequisites

Ability to identify who is speaking and group membership in context. Co-teach pronoun recognition and any relevant agreement. Case and possessive variants are extensions. Requirements vary by outcome and depth. Use contextual audio and glosses for oral tasks so learners can demonstrate the target without script literacy.

## 13. Mastery evidence

Change the speaker and group membership in two reviewed dialogues. Ask for the actual referent and then for original solo and group responses. Observe pronoun/reference evidence independently of morphology.
