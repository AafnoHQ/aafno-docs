## 2. Learning outcomes

- LO1: Identify a place cue.
- LO2: Distinguish location from destination.
- LO3: Resolve viewpoint or reference location.
- LO4: Distinguish standalone place use from a relational expression.
- LO5: Produce a contextually appropriate place cue.

## 3. Meaning and usage

A spatial adverb situates an action or state. The same place word can be heard with a static or directional reading depending on the predicate and viewpoint.

## 4. Formation rules

Establish the speaker’s position on a map. Compare an invitation to come here with a statement locating an object there. Then use upstairs with a motion verb and compare a phrase whose reference object is a table.

**Worked models.** यहाँ आऊ। / Yahaã aau. / Come here. (Destination at the speaker’s location; familiar address.) Compare किताब त्यहाँ छ। / Kitaab tyahaã chha. / The book is there. (Static position at a known point away from speaker.) The examples are instructional candidates.

## 5. Variations and agreement

Spatial anchors, speaker movement and the predicate may change interpretation. Relational marking belongs to G14 and questions such as where belong to their reconciled G17 targets.

## 8. Anticipated mistakes and corrective feedback

A learner can sound plausible while using a cue unrelated to the target. Test what they inferred with the two independent prompts in section 12.

**Corrective cue for the first prompt.** Trace the motion on the map, then mark the stationary object separately.

**Corrective cue for the second prompt.** If the cue points to someone else’s location, move the speaker marker back to your position and retry.

Use the [variant-aware assessment policy](../proficiency-framework.md#variant-aware-assessment) if context permits more than one answer or the variant has not been reviewed.

## 12. Activity types

References to rows are to section 6. Provide Devanagari, Romanisation and English by default. For unaided production, hide examples and target expressions until after the response. A scaffolded response is recorded as guided practice.

### First evidence target

- **Learner prompt.** In rows 1 and 2, which place is a destination?
- **Expected response.** Row 1. Row 2 locates the book.
- **Assessment target.** LO2 only. Score this response independently of the other activities.
- **Feedback and retry.** Trace the motion on the map, then mark the stationary object separately.
- **Validation status.** Draft. Confirm naturalness, context and acceptable responses before scored use.

### Second evidence target

- **Learner prompt.** Tell a familiar listener to come to your position. Supply the Nepali place cue without the example list.
- **Expected response.** यहाँ / Yahaã / here.
- **Assessment target.** LO5 only. Score this response independently of the other activities.
- **Feedback and retry.** If the cue points to someone else’s location, move the speaker marker back to your position and retry.

## 13. Mastery evidence
